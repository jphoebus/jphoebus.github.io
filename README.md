# Joshua Phoebus: State Policy Analysis

Side-by-side comparisons of how states make decisions when a new industry outgrows the existing rules: data centers, energy, infrastructure, payments, and health care.

**Homepage:** [jphoebus.github.io](https://jphoebus.github.io/)

## Data centers and the grid

- [Which data center load counts](https://jphoebus.github.io/large-load-screening/): How six PJM states screen data center electricity requests before they count in the load forecast: forecast review, contract terms, collateral, and duplicate-request disclosure.
- [Who pays for data center power](https://jphoebus.github.io/large-load-tariffs/): How states set the terms large electricity customers must accept: size thresholds, minimum bills, contract terms, exit fees, and collateral.
- [What states changed on data centers in 2026](https://jphoebus.github.io/data-center-legislation-2026/): What legislatures, governors, utility regulators, and local governments changed this year, and where each change came from.

## Incentives and accountability

- [State data center incentives, compared](https://jphoebus.github.io/state-incentive-comparison/): How states structure their data center sales tax exemptions: eligibility, investment and job requirements, and duration.
- [What counts as exempt data center equipment](https://jphoebus.github.io/data-center-exempt-equipment/): Which equipment qualifies under each state's data center sales tax exemption.
- [How states check their tax incentives](https://jphoebus.github.io/tax-incentive-evaluation/): How states evaluate whether their tax incentives deliver what they promise.

## Infrastructure and what comes next

- [DOE's SPARK grid selections, tracked](https://jphoebus.github.io/spark-grid-tracker/): The Department of Energy's SPARK grid-upgrade selections, tracked as they move forward.
- [Are state rules ready for quantum computing?](https://jphoebus.github.io/quantum-readiness/): Whether state data center incentives, tariffs, and reporting rules address quantum computing, and how states are preparing.

## Payments and consumer finance

- [Is it a loan? How states set payments rules](https://jphoebus.github.io/state-payments-policy/): How eleven states classify earned wage access, how a federal bill would change state authority, and how Illinois and Colorado handled card fees on sales tax and tips.

## Health care and pharmaceuticals

- [Who sets the price of a prescription?](https://jphoebus.github.io/state-drug-supply-chain/): How five states regulate pharmacy benefit managers, drug prices, and 340B contract pharmacy arrangements.
- [Who arranges the ride?](https://jphoebus.github.io/medicaid-transportation/): How five Northeast states run Medicaid non-emergency medical transportation: delivery models, broker contracts, procurement, and 2025–26 legislation.
